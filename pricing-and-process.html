The Process

From first meeting to a working assistant.

Most clients start with a single use case, such as invoicing or scheduling. Here is how that goes, step by step.

  1. We meet

    In person anywhere in the Lower Mainland, or by video or phone. We learn how your business runs: your inbox, your tools, and where your time goes.

  2. We choose your first use case

    We narrow the list down to the one task that gives you the most back, and plan the later ones at the same time. That way the first choices we make never get in the way of what you add next.

  3. We research and choose the tools

    We test the options first. Where an existing tool does the job, we set that up around your workflow. Where nothing fits, we build something from scratch.

  4. Deposit, then we start

    Work begins once the deposit is in. The deposit is credited toward the final bill for that phase.

  5. We integrate it

    We connect the assistant to the tools you already use, such as your email, calendar and invoicing, so nothing about how you work has to change.

  6. We test it on your real work

    Your own invoices, emails and schedule, before anything goes live. Nothing is sent without your approval.

  7. We finish, then final payment

    You review the result. When it works the way you expect, you pay the balance for that phase.

  8. Then, if it is paying off, the next use case

    Each new integration repeats the same steps. You are never committed to more than the phase in front of you.

Questions

Common questions.

How much does it cost to set up an AI assistant for a small business?

Most single-task setups, such as invoicing, start around $1,200 after a $300 to $500 discovery deposit. Larger projects with several integrations or custom builds go up to about $3,500. You see the figure in writing before work begins.

Do I have to commit to everything up front?

No. Each phase is its own decision. You pay a deposit, we build and test, you approve, and you pay the balance. Then you choose whether to continue.

Do I need new software, or does it work with what I already use?

It works with what you already use, such as Gmail and Google Calendar. We only suggest new tools when they clearly save you time.

Will it send anything without my approval?

No. By default the assistant drafts and you approve before anything goes out.

Is it safe to give an AI assistant access to my business email?

It can be, if it is set up carefully. We limit what it can access, keep your data in your own accounts, and explain exactly what it can and cannot do before you agree.

Can you meet in person?

Yes, anywhere in the Lower Mainland. We can also work by video or phone.

What if I only need help picking a tool?

That works. Many people only need a recommendation and a short setup session. We will tell you honestly if an existing tool is all you need.
