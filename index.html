How We Work

Systems built around how your business actually runs.

Every build starts with your real operation: your inbox, your files, your tools. We write a knowledge base about your business, and the system acts on what it's confident about, brings you the decision when it's not, and learns from every correction. Here is how a typical system runs.

01
Event comes in
New email, message, or scheduled trigger
02
Agent reads your knowledge base
Routing rules, contacts, projects, processes
03
Acts or asks
Confident? Handles it. Unsure? Asks you first
04
System learns
Your corrections update the knowledge base

Built on Claude and OpenAI, with custom API and webhook integrations across email, calendar, CRM, and cloud storage. Prompt engineering and confidence-based routing decide what to handle and what to bring you.

Oversight & Safety

Starts in approval mode

Every system begins with human sign-off on every action. It only acts independently once you trust it.

Confidence routing

High confidence: acts silently. Medium: acts and flags for review. Low: asks before doing anything.

Full visibility

Every action is logged. You can see exactly what was done, when, and why, and correct anything instantly.

Your data stays yours

Systems connect through scoped, revocable permissions to accounts you already control, on established enterprise infrastructure. Your data stays in your systems and is never used to train AI models.

In Practice

One example of what we've built, in full. The integrations and the purpose change with every business; the approach stays the same.

Residential Development Company — Vancouver, BC

From inbox to the right budget line in a single tap.

The problem

Invoices arrived by email across multiple active projects. Each one meant reading the paperwork, pulling out the details, entering them into the right budget by hand, and hoping overruns got noticed before they became expensive.

What we built

A complete invoice and budget-tracking system. Invoices arrive by email, appear in the owner's chat within seconds with the details already extracted, and land in the right project budget with a single tap.

The result

Budgets stay current in real time, with automatic warnings the moment any budget line runs hot. The owner makes the call. The system does the paperwork around it.
