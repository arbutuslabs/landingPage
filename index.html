In Practice

One recent build, told in full. The integrations and the purpose change with every business; the approach stays the same.

Residential Development Company — Vancouver, BC

From inbox to the right budget line — in a single tap.

The problem

Invoices arrived by email across multiple active projects. Each one meant reading the paperwork, pulling out the details, entering them into the right budget by hand — and hoping overruns got noticed before they became expensive.

What we built

A complete invoice and budget-tracking system. Invoices arrive by email, appear in the owner's chat within seconds with the details already extracted, and land in the right project budget with a single tap.

The result

Budgets stay current in real time, with automatic warnings the moment any budget line runs hot. The owner makes the call — the system does the paperwork around it.
