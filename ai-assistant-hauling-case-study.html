Case Study

An AI assistant that invoices and schedules for a hauling company.

A Lower Mainland hauling company spent its evenings turning job emails into invoices and juggling bookings between loads. We set up an AI assistant that drafts every invoice from the job email, books jobs, handles texts, and takes voice requests from the truck. The owner approves everything before it goes out.

Industry

Hauling and logistics

Location

Lower Mainland, BC

Main job

Invoicing from email

Approach

Existing tools, set up and customized

The Problem

Paperwork after a full day of driving.

Every job arrived by email. Each one had to be read, typed into an invoice by hand, and sent, usually at the end of a long day. Bookings, client texts and follow-ups all had to wait until the truck was parked, and overdue invoices were easy to lose track of.

The owner did not want new software to learn. He wanted the tools he already used to do more of the work.

What We Set Up

An assistant built around how the business already runs.

Invoices drafted from job emails

When a job email comes in, the assistant pulls out the details and drafts the invoice in the company's own template, ready to approve.

Overdue checks and summaries

Scheduled checks flag unpaid invoices and send a short summary, so nothing slips.

Voice requests from the truck

Using a Siri Shortcut, the owner asks the assistant for what he needs, hands free, and hears the reply read aloud.

Booking and reminders

Jobs go straight into Google Calendar, with reminders set automatically.

Texts with drafted replies

Client texts come in with a reply already drafted. The owner approves it and it sends.

Approval before anything is sent

The assistant drafts and prepares. Nothing goes to a client until the owner says so.

How It Works

From job email to sent invoice.

01

04

The invoice goes out and is watched until it is paid.

Our Approach

Existing tools first, built in phases.

We met in person, mapped how jobs and invoices actually moved through the business, and tested on real invoices before anything went live. Instead of building new software, we set up and customized tools the company could keep using on its own accounts.

GmailGoogle CalendarSiri ShortcutsTwilio text messagingChatGPT or Claude

The work was delivered one phase at a time, starting with invoicing. Each phase was its own decision, and software and text fees are paid directly by the company at cost.

The Result

Invoices go out without the evening paperwork.

Invoices are drafted the moment a job email lands, bookings and texts are handled from the road, and overdue invoices are flagged automatically. The owner keeps every decision. The assistant does the typing.

Approve an invoice by voice from the truck, and it is out before the next load.

Questions

Common questions about this setup.

Can an AI assistant create invoices from my emails?

Yes. It reads each job email, pulls out the details and drafts an invoice in your own template. You approve it before it is sent.

Can I use it by voice while I drive?

Yes. A Siri Shortcut lets you make requests hands free and hear the reply read aloud, so you can approve or ask for things without picking up your phone.

Does it send anything without my approval?

No. The assistant drafts invoices and replies. Nothing goes out until you approve it.

Do I need new software?

No. It works with tools you likely already use, such as Gmail and Google Calendar. Subscriptions stay in your name and you pay them at cost.

What does a setup like this cost?

A single use case such as invoicing starts from about $1,200 after a discovery deposit, and larger setups with several integrations go up to about $3,500. See pricing and process for details.

Do you work with hauling companies outside Vancouver?

Yes. We meet in person anywhere in the Lower Mainland, including Surrey, Langley, Burnaby, Richmond and Coquitlam, and work remotely across BC.
